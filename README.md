# Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze 500 retail sales transactions from 2025, including revenue, profit, products, regions, and monthly sales trends.

## Dashboard Preview
![Sales Performance Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-10-11%20143444.png)

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Revenue: 374.70K
- Total Profit: 134.74K
- Profit Margin: 35.96%
- Total Orders: 500

## Key Business Insights
- Electronics generated the highest category revenue.
- East was the highest-revenue region at approximately 94K.
- Accessories had the highest profit margin at 55.63%.
- December recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately 48K.
- Laptop was the highest-revenue product at approximately 117K.

## Tools and Skills
Microsoft Power BI, DAX, Data Visualization, KPI Reporting, Interactive Slicers, and Business Analysis.

## Dataset
500 retail sales transactions covering 2025.

## Project Objective
Transform retail sales data into useful business insights to support decision-making.
